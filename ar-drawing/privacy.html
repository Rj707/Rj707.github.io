Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Drawing: Trace to Sketch ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your data and permissions. Your privacy is fundamental to our development philosophy: all core features function 100% on your device without remote servers.

1. Camera Permission Disclosure (Live Optical Overlay)

AR Drawing utilizes your device camera solely as an optical viewfinder to display semi-transparent drawing outlines over real physical paper. We adhere to the strictest on-device processing standards:

  • Zero Remote Transmission: Your live camera stream is rendered purely in memory on your local hardware. No video frames, camera feeds, or live imagery are ever transmitted across the internet, recorded secretly, or stored on remote cloud servers.
  • Flashlight / Torch Control: Camera torch toggling is used purely to illuminate your real-world drawing paper in dim environments.

2. Photo Library & Gallery Permission (Photo-to-Stencil)

When you use the "Import Photo" feature to convert personal pictures or anime into trace outlines:

  • Local Edge-Detection: The photo is processed strictly on your phone using local grayscale and Sobel/Canny edge-detection algorithms.
  • Private Storage: Your personal photos never leave your device. We do not maintain any cloud databases or AI model training servers that store your photos.

3. Zero User Account Collection

We believe in frictionless creative tools. You are never required to register an account, sign in with social media, provide an email address, or enter your name to use AR Drawing. All templates, streak milestones, and saved project states reside in sandboxed local storage (iOS UserDefaults / Android SharedPreferences).

4. In-App Purchases & Advertising Disclosures

  • StoreKit & Google Play Billing: Purchases (such as "Lifetime Pro Pass" or "Ad-Free") are handled exclusively by Apple and Google. We do not have access to your credit card, bank details, or billing address.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Consent: We integrate Google AdMob to deliver non-intrusive advertisements in the free tier. In the EU, UK, and California, Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) is displayed to gather explicit user consent in accordance with GDPR and CCPA. Purchasing the Pro Pass permanently removes all advertisements.

5. Data Deletion & Storage Control

Since your project files and drawing history are stored entirely on your local device, deleting the app from your device permanently removes 100% of cached templates, saved outlines, and preferences instantly.
