Privacy Policy GDPR • COPPA • CalOPPA

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Your privacy is paramount. AR Drawing ("the App", developed by Saad / Rj707) is built strictly around local-first privacy principles. We believe your creative tools should never monitor, track, or commercialize your personal identity.

1. Device Camera & Photos Privacy

AR Drawing uses the camera and photo library exclusively for real-time augmented reality sketching:

  • Camera Usage: Real-time camera feed is processed strictly on-device to project your chosen stencil outline onto paper. The live video feed is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted to external servers.
  • Photo Library Access: Used only when you choose to import an image to trace, or when you explicitly tap to save your completed artwork to your camera roll. Photos are never uploaded to any remote cloud.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

AR Drawing does not collect personal information such as your name, email, address, or phone number. We utilize industry-standard, anonymized telemetry frameworks:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous crash stack traces to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Aggregates non-identifiable usage metrics (e.g. app opens, session duration) to optimize app performance.
  • Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing: Manages In-App Purchases (such as Lifetime Passes) securely through your platform store account. We never see or store your payment details.

3. Advertising & Ad Safety Standard

We enforce a strict Zero-Vulgarity Clean Ads Standard via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). All ad requests are code-restricted to MaxAdContentRating.g (General Audiences suitable for all ages). Sensitive categories including dating, gambling, and mature content are strictly blocked.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We are fully committed to protecting children's digital safety under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). AR Drawing does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All drawing templates and guided lessons are safe and family-friendly.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all sketches and settings are saved locally on your device, deleting the app will immediately remove all locally stored preferences and progress.
