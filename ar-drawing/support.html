Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I set up my phone to draw without a tripod?
Rest your phone flat across the top of any tall drinking glass, mug, or water bottle. Let the camera lens peek slightly over the rim pointing down at a blank sheet of paper on your desk. You will see the stencil floating on your phone screen!
❓ My drawing shifted when I accidentally touched the screen. How do I fix it?
Tap the Lock to Draw button (padlock icon) on the toolbar before drawing. This locks all touch inputs so resting your hand or palm on the screen won't move or resize your outline!
❓ Can I convert my own photos into sketches?
Yes! Tap the "Import Photo" button on the home screen, select any picture from your gallery, and choose between Outline, High Contrast, or Detailed edge filters.
❓ How do I restore my Lifetime VIP purchase?
Open the VIP Pass screen and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom. As long as you are logged into the same Apple ID or Google Play account used during purchase, your lifetime access will be restored instantly with zero charges.
