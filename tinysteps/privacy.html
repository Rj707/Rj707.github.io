Privacy Policy & COPPA Compliance

🔒 Apple Kids Category (Ages 5 and Under) & COPPA Certified
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

TinySteps is built with strict privacy-by-design for early learners. We do not collect, store, transmit, harvest, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children or adults. No accounts, registrations, emails, names, or device contacts are ever requested.

2. On-Device Local Storage

All child tracing progress, unlocked characters, star counts, and difficulty settings remain strictly on your local device within the sandboxed operating system environment. We operate no cloud databases or remote servers holding user data.

3. No Cross-App Behavioral Tracking

TinySteps complies strictly with Apple's Kids Category policies. We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) access, do not read the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), and do not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Child-Directed Contextual Advertising

The free trial tier uses Google AdMob configured in strict Child-Directed Treatment mode (COPPA compliant). Advertisements are strictly contextual and family-safe. Behavioral retargeting and user profiling are prohibited. Families upgrading to Lifetime VIP enjoy permanent, 100% ad removal.

5. StoreKit 2 & Google Play Billing

Financial transactions for in-app purchases are executed securely through Apple StoreKit 2 and Google Play Billing. TinySteps never receives, stores, or handles credit card or billing details.

6. Parental Gate Protection

All outbound links, support contact buttons, and store purchase sheets are locked behind a certified adult Parental Gate (randomized multiplication/addition challenge) to ensure children cannot leave the app or make unintentional purchases.
