Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my Lifetime VIP Pass?
    Open the Parents Zone by tapping the gear icon on the Home screen and solving the adult math gate. Tap "Restore Purchases". If you previously purchased Lifetime VIP with your Apple ID or Google Play account, it will unlock automatically.
  • How do I change the tracing guidance or touch tolerance?
    You can change the visual guidance at any time while tracing by tapping the guide pill in the top right corner (✍️ Guide: Full ➔ 🌫️ Guide: Faded ➔ 🧠 Freehand). Touch tolerance (Gentle, Standard, School-Ready) can be configured in the Parents Zone settings.
  • Can my child use the app without internet?
    Yes! TinySteps is completely functional offline. All sounds, animations, and letters are built directly into the app.
