✨ About TinySteps

TinySteps is a sensory-focused handwriting and phonics learning application designed specifically for toddlers, preschoolers, and early kindergarteners (Ages 2–5). Featuring Montessori stroke rhymes, tactile multisensory haptics, sparkling completion animations, and progressive visual scaffolding (Full Guidance, Ghost Faded, and Freehand), TinySteps guides early learners from their first touch to confident independent writing.

  • All 26 Uppercase & Lowercase Letters: True pedagogical stroke directions with numbered start circles and rhythmic cues.
  • Numbers 1–9 & Star Counting: Dynamic quantity recognition and interactive tactile counters.
  • Visual Guidance Scaffolding: Easily switch between Full Guide, Faded (Ghost Track), and Freehand from memory.
  • Certified Parental Gate: Adult-only access to all settings, external links, and store purchases.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At TinySteps, child privacy, safety, and security are our highest priorities. TinySteps is built in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and Apple App Store Guideline 1.3 (Kids Category) & 5.1.1 (Data Collection).

1. Zero Collection of Personal Information

We do not collect, request, transmit, harvest, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children or parents. You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data to use TinySteps.

2. On-Device Local Progress Storage

All learning progress, completed letters, collected stars, and difficulty preferences are stored exclusively on your local device (using local sandboxed storage). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service.

3. No Behavioral Tracking or Analytics SDKs

TinySteps contains zero third-party behavioral analytics, profiling, or tracking software. We do not use the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, because our app does not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Child-Directed Contextual Advertising

The free trial tier of TinySteps utilizes Google AdMob configured in strict Child-Directed Treatment mode (tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true) and Under Age of Consent mode. All ads served are strictly non-personalized, family-safe, and contextual. No behavioral profiling or retargeting is permitted. Families who upgrade to the Lifetime VIP Pass enjoy a permanent, 100% ad-free experience.

5. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2 & Google Play)

All in-app purchases (such as the one-time Lifetime VIP unlock) are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2 or Google Play Billing. TinySteps does not collect, receive, or store any payment or financial information.

6. Protected Parental Gate

In full accordance with Apple Kids Category rules, all external links, support email triggers, settings menus, and store purchase buttons are secured behind a certified adult Parental Gate (randomized multiplication/addition mathematical challenge) to prevent accidental taps by young children.

7. Contact Developer Privacy Officer

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or your child's safety, please contact developer support directly at rajasaad707@gmail.com.

💬 Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Need help or have a suggestion? We're dedicated to making TinySteps the best early learning experience for your family.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • How do I restore my Lifetime VIP Purchase?
    Open the Parents Zone (tap the gear icon on the Home screen and solve the adult math gate). Tap "Restore Purchases". Your Apple ID or Google Play account will verify your entitlement instantly without recharging you.
  • How do I turn on Freehand or Faded lines?
    While practicing any letter or number, tap the quick toggle badge in the top right corner of the screen (✍️ Guide: Full ➔ 🌫️ Guide: Faded ➔ 🧠 Freehand), or adjust it inside the Parents Zone.
  • Does the app work offline?
    Yes! All letter models, handwriting animations, phonics audio, and number games are bundled inside the app and function 100% offline without requiring internet access.
