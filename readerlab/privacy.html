Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

Reader Lab is designed with privacy by design. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, or phone numbers to use the application.

2. On-Device Data Storage

All imported reading documents, custom text snippets, speed reading telemetry (WPM, words read, reading duration), and user preferences (selected themes, dyslexia fonts, color tints) remain strictly on your local device via local Apple storage sandboxes.

3. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

All financial transactions, subscriptions, and lifetime unlock purchases are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. We do not receive, store, or have access to your credit card details or billing information.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob

Reader Lab utilizes Google AdMob to serve non-personalized advertisements for non-VIP users. AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers solely for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud detection in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Users who upgrade to Reader Lab VIP enjoy permanent, 100% ad removal.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Reader Lab does not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for all audiences.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact developer support at rajasaad707@gmail.com.
