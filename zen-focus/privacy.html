Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

Zen Focus is built on a strict privacy-first foundation. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, or phone numbers to use any feature of the application.

2. 100% Local On-Device Storage

All user records—including completed focus sessions, daily streaks, timer durations, sound volume preferences, and chime toggles—are saved exclusively on your local device using standard on-device sandboxed storage. Your productivity data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote servers.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2 & Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases, including the Zen Pro Lifetime one-time unlock, are processed directly and securely by Apple (via StoreKit 2) or Google (via Google Play Billing). We do not receive, process, or store your credit card information, financial data, or billing address.

4. Offline Audio & Local Notifications

All ambient soundscapes and Tibetan singing bowl chimes are bundled directly within the app binary and play 100% locally. Session completion alerts utilize local on-device notifications without requiring any remote push notification servers or device tokens.

5. Diagnostics & Stability

The application may utilize industry-standard, privacy-preserving crash reporting (such as Firebase Crashlytics) strictly to identify and resolve software crashes. No personal user data, keystrokes, or session histories are collected or attached to crash reports.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Zen Focus does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for audiences of all ages.

7. Contact Developer Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to developer support at rajasaad707@gmail.com.
