About the App

Zen Focus is an intentional, distraction-free productivity chronometer crafted to eradicate the friction, subscription traps, and social clutter of typical timer apps. Engineered for ADHD focus, study marathons, deep programming sessions, and mindful deep work, Zen Focus pairs an intuitive rotary dial with a 6-channel offline sound generator to lock you into effortless flow states.

  • Instant Start: Zero accounts, no email logins, and zero onboarding menus. Start focusing in under 3 seconds.
  • 100% Offline: All 6+ ambient soundscapes (rain, brown noise, cozy fireplace, forest wind, coffee shop, ocean waves) are bundled locally on your device.
  • OLED Pure Black: Genuine #000000 battery-optimized aesthetic engineered to reduce eye strain.
  • Anti-Subscription Model: Built on a single $4.99 One-Time Lifetime Unlock. No recurring monthly or yearly subscription fees ever.
