123 Numbers: Count & Tracing

🔒 COPPA & Google Play Families Certified — Ages 5 and Under
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Children's Online Privacy Protection)

123 Numbers: Count & Tracing is built strictly for young learners. We do not collect, store, transmit, harvest, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children or parents. No accounts, logins, emails, names, phone numbers, or contacts are ever requested.

2. On-Device Local Sandbox Storage

All counting progress, unlocked numbers, balloon pop scores, and star badges are saved exclusively on your local device. We run zero cloud tracking databases.

3. No Cross-App Behavioral Tracking or Advertising IDs

We strictly comply with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies. We have removed the Advertising ID (AAID) and Privacy Sandbox permissions from our application manifests. We do not track children across other apps or websites.

4. Child-Directed Contextual Advertising

The free version utilizes Google AdMob configured in strict Child-Directed Treatment mode (TagForChildDirectedTreatment: true) with a maximum ad rating of G (General Audience). Advertisements are purely contextual and strictly capped (at most once every 4 completed levels). In-app purchase for Lifetime Pro permanently eliminates all ads.

5. Secure Purchases via Official App Stores

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple App Store (StoreKit) and Google Play Billing. Our app never accesses or stores credit card or billing credentials.

6. Mandatory Parental Gate

In-app purchase prompts and external links are strictly guarded by an adult Parental Gate requiring parents to solve a randomized math problem before proceeding.
