🛠️ Support & Help Center Player Assistance

Welcome to the official support portal for Slide Routes: Brain Puzzle. Below you will find answers to common questions, gameplay guides, and troubleshooting steps.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is the primary objective of Slide Routes?

Your goal is to guide the primary golden block (marked with a star ★) across the orthogonal grid to reach the glowing green exit gate located on the right boundary. Later advanced levels introduce dual target blocks (Amber ★ and Cyan ◆) where both must reach their matching exit gates.

Q2: How do the Dual-Mode Controls work?

Slide Routes offers two complementary control schemes tailored to your playstyle:

  • Fluid Drag Gestures: Touch any block and drag along its allowed orientation (horizontal blocks slide left/right, vertical blocks slide up/down).
  • Directional D-Pad & Tap Mode: Prefer precision? Use the directional keypad or tap any piece to reveal available movement corridors, then tap your destination cell.

Q3: How do the AI BFS Hints work?

Unlike simple recorded replay videos, Slide Routes features a real-time, deterministic Breadth-First Search (BFS) solver built into the app engine. When you tap the Hint (💡) button, the engine analyzes your current board position and highlights the exact optimal next move required to reach the exit in the fewest remaining steps.

Q4: How do I restore my "Remove Ads (Pro)" purchase?

If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new device:

  • Ensure you are logged into the same Apple ID (iOS) or Google Account (Android) used for the initial purchase.
  • Open Slide Routes and navigate to the Settings (⚙️) screen.
  • Tap "Restore Purchases". The store will immediately re-verify your entitlement and reinstate your Pro ad-free benefits at no extra charge.

Q5: Can I play completely offline?

Yes, 100%! All 60 handcrafted levels, game logic, local save states, and BFS solver algorithms are bundled inside the app binary. You can play anywhere—including airplanes, subways, and remote areas—without an internet connection.

Q6: Are there countdown timers or move limits?

No. Slide Routes is engineered as a relaxing, cognitive spatial puzzle. There are no ticking countdown clocks, no heart/energy meters limiting your play, and you have 100% free, unlimited undo operations to experiment without penalty.

Q7: How do I reset all my progress?

Open Settings (⚙️) and select "Reset Progress". This will wipe all locally stored star ratings, move records, and board states, returning the game to its initial state.
