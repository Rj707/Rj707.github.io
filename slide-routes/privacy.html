🔒 Privacy Policy GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy discloses how Slide Routes: Brain Puzzle ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, handles, and protects your information.

1. Zero User Account Collection

We believe in frictionless, private puzzle entertainment. We do NOT require user accounts, logins, passwords, names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise physical location. You can launch and master all 60 stages of Slide Routes anonymously without creating a profile or providing any personal identity documents.

2. On-Device Game Data & 100% Offline Gameplay

All game progress—including your completed stages (1 to 60), fewest moves records, 3-star efficiency ratings, active board save states, and audio settings—is stored strictly on your local device via sandboxed local storage (iOS UserDefaults / Android SharedPreferences):

  • Your puzzle state and solutions are calculated entirely on your device using an offline Breadth-First Search (BFS) solver.
  • No gameplay telemetry, board states, or solve histories are transmitted to any external game servers.
  • The game operates 100% offline in Airplane Mode with zero network connectivity required.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit & Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads (Pro)") are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit and Google via Google Play Billing. We never have access to, nor do we collect or store, your payment credentials, credit card details, billing address, or store account passwords. All billing transactions are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Diagnostic Metrics (Google AdMob)

To support continuous development and maintain a free-to-play tier, Slide Routes integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve respectful, non-intrusive advertisements:

  • Consent Management (UMP): In jurisdictions requiring user consent (such as the EU/EEA and UK under GDPR, and California under CCPA), we present Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before serving personalized ads.
  • Strict Pacing Rules: Interstitial ads are never shown during the first 5 levels or first 5 minutes of gameplay. Furthermore, interstitials require a mandatory 180-second cooldown and at least 3 completed levels between impressions.
  • Data Handled by AdMob: The ad network may collect ephemeral device identifiers (such as IDFA or Google Advertising ID where consented), coarse IP address for regional routing, and crash/diagnostic logs solely for fraud detection and ad delivery.
  • Complete Ad Removal (Pro): Purchasing "Remove Ads (Pro)" completely deactivates ad initialization, permanently eliminating 100% of banner and interstitial ads, while unlocking unlimited instant solver hints.
  • For more information on Google's advertising practices, please review the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

5. Audience & Child Privacy

Slide Routes is designed for a general audience (ages 13 and older). The puzzle content contains zero violence, blood, gambling, or adult themes, earning general audience content ratings (ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, Apple 4+). The app does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately.

6. User Data Deletion Instructions

Because Slide Routes does not maintain remote user accounts or store personal records in cloud databases, managing or wiping your data is entirely under your control:

  • Instant In-App Reset: Open the game → tap the Settings (⚙️) icon → tap "Reset Progress". This immediately purges all saved stages, stars, move counts, and local cache.
  • Device Uninstallation: Deleting the app from your iOS or Android device permanently removes all sandboxed data from your filesystem.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. Any modifications will be posted to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
