🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Microphone audio is measured on your device and is not recorded or sent anywhere. The app can send crash reports and simple counts, such as when you start the meter, save a session, or play a tone. It also shows ads.

  • Microphone audio: Sound is measured in real time on your device. DecibelPro does not record, save, or send microphone audio.
  • Crash reports and usage counts: The app can send a crash report if it closes unexpectedly, and a simple count when you start the meter, save a session, or play a tone. Those reports do not include microphone audio, decibel readings, or your notes.
  • No account: DecibelPro does not ask you to create an account or enter your name or email.
  • Advertisements: We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may use a device advertising identifier, such as the iPhone IDFA, if you allow tracking.
  • Session history: Saved measurements and notes stay on your device.
