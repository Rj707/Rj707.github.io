📲 How to Add Widgets to Your Home Screen

🍎 Apple iOS (iPhone & iPad)

  1. Touch and hold any empty area on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap the + button in the top corner to open the Widget Gallery.
  3. Search for DecibelPro and tap it.
  4. Swipe to choose Measure Sound (Small) or Last Session (Medium), then tap Add Widget.

🤖 Android Phones & Tablets

  1. Touch and hold an empty space on your home screen, then tap Widgets.
  2. Scroll to DecibelPro and drag your preferred widget onto your home screen.
  3. Quick Shortcut: You can also open DecibelPro → Settings → Home Screen Widgets to pin widgets directly on supported launchers.

💡 Widget Information & Troubleshooting

  • Saved Readings vs. Live Audio: Displayed decibel readings reflect your previously completed and saved measurement records. The widgets do not run continuous background audio recording, perform stealth listening, or drain battery life while your phone is idle.
  • Missing Widget in Picker: If you don't see DecibelPro in your widget list immediately after updating, open the DecibelPro app at least once so the operating system registers the widget extension, or perform a quick restart of your launcher.
  • Empty or Stale Sessions: If the widget displays "No saved sessions yet," simply open the app, tap Start Meter, and save a measurement. If you delete a session or clear your history in Settings, the widget automatically updates to reflect the change.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to our product. DecibelPro is designed to be completely safe, transparent, and respectful of your data:

  • Microphone Audio: DecibelPro processes microphone sound levels strictly in real-time on your device to compute amplitude and decibel measurements. We do not record, save, store, or transmit your audio anywhere.
  • Personal Information: DecibelPro does not require user registration, login accounts, emails, phone numbers, or identity collection.
  • Advertisements: We use Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive sponsor ads for the free tier. AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (such as IDFA under Apple's AppTrackingTransparency on iOS and Google Advertising ID on Android) in full compliance with store privacy guidelines.
  • Family-Safe Advertising Standards: In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play Developer Policies, we strictly enforce category-blocking controls within Google AdMob to maintain a clean, respectful, and family-appropriate experience. We actively prohibit and filter out advertisements featuring sexually suggestive material, nudity, adult themes, dating services, and gambling. Should any ad inadvertently bypass these filters, users can report it via our support contact for immediate review and advertiser exclusion.
  • Local Storage: Your measurement history, calibration offsets, and theme preferences are stored exclusively on your device using local storage.
  • Home Screen Widgets & Local Data Sharing: DecibelPro provides optional home screen widgets on iOS and Android. To display your latest saved measurement summary on the "Last Session" widget, the app securely shares a minimal data payload locally between the app and the widget extension via platform-native shared storage (Apple App Group sandbox on iOS; private application SharedPreferences on Android):
    • Shared Fields: Only non-sensitive summary metadata is shared locally: (1) internal session ID string (id), (2) computed average sound level (averageDb), (3) peak/maximum sound level (peakDb), and (4) session start timestamp (epochMillis).
    • No Audio or Server Uploads: Widget data sharing is strictly on-device. Widgets do not continuously access the microphone, do not record or stream audio in the background, and do not upload session summaries or device data to any remote server or third party.
    • Session Deletion & Clear History: When you delete a measurement session or use "Clear All History" in Settings, the shared widget cache is immediately updated or cleared from device storage, resetting the widget to "No saved sessions yet."
