🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Your privacy is our highest priority. DecibelPro is designed from the ground up to be private, transparent, and secure:

  • Microphone Audio (100% On-Device): DecibelPro analyzes sound pressure levels strictly in real time directly on your device. We never record, store, upload, or transmit any audio data to any server.
  • Zero Personal Data: DecibelPro does not require account creation, logins, emails, names, or personal identities to use.
  • Advertisements: We use Google AdMob to deliver advertisements. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as IDFA) in full compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework to display relevant ads.
  • Session History: All sound level history records are stored exclusively inside your device's local storage.
