Reader Lab

Neuro-Adaptive RSVP Speed Reading & Dyslexia Accessibility for iOS

About the App

Reader Lab is an advanced speed-reading and reading accessibility tool engineered for high comprehension and visual stamina. Using Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) with scientific Optimal Recognition Point (ORP) tracking, Bionic Reading fixation bolding, and scotopic Irlen tint overlays, Reader Lab lets you read up to 3x faster without cognitive fatigue.
