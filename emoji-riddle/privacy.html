🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Emoji Riddle — Brain Teaser ("the App", bundle and package identifier com.saadapps.emojiriddle), distributed on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, manages data. It distinguishes between game progress stored locally on your device and data transmitted over the internet by integrated third-party services.

1. Account Requirements & Personal Identification

The App does not require user account registration, profile creation, or logins. The developer does not maintain a custom remote user database and does not ask you to enter directly identifying personal contact information, such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or telephone number.

However, when your device connects to the internet, third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the App process and transmit network and device telemetry as detailed below.

2. On-Device Local Game Data

Core gameplay progress is stored locally on your device using standard operating system storage frameworks (UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android):

  • Solved puzzle level indices across Classic Mode and Junior Mode.
  • In-game virtual coin balances and hint inventory.
  • Winning streaks (tracked via the local counter player_streak, representing consecutive riddle solves without skipping).
  • Appearance preferences (theme selection) and sound effect toggles.
  • Local timestamp records for daily bonus hint replenishment.

This game progression data is maintained on your physical device. It is not transmitted to a developer-hosted user account server.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Data Transmission

The App integrates third-party SDKs from Google to provide crash diagnostics, gameplay analytics, and in-game advertisements. When your device is connected to the internet, these SDKs transmit technical data to external servers:

A. Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

  • Verified In-App Behavior: The App logs pseudonymous gameplay telemetry, including completed level events (level_completed with level index and game mode) and hint usage events (hint_used with hint type). If an unhandled application crash or exception occurs, Firebase Crashlytics records stack traces, OS version, device hardware specifications, and system timestamps.
  • Network Transmission: These events and crash traces are transmitted securely over HTTPS to Google Firebase servers to monitor stability and level difficulty balance.
  • Vendor Policies: For details on Google's technical handling, refer to the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

B. Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • Verified In-App Behavior: In Classic Mode and Junior Word Builder, the App displays standard banner advertisements, interstitial transitions, and optional rewarded hint videos. In Junior Picture Play mode, ad requests and ad displays are suppressed in code.
  • Content Rating Configuration: The App's code explicitly sets the maximum advertising content rating parameter to General Audiences (MaxAdContentRating.g in the iOS Google Mobile Ads SDK and RequestConfiguration.MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G in Android). Sensitive categories (such as gambling, mature content, and dating) are also designated as blocked in AdMob account settings.
  • Inherent Network Processing: Serving mobile advertisements requires transmitting device network requests (including device IP addresses) to ad servers. Depending on user consent choices and operating system privacy controls (such as Apple App Tracking Transparency on iOS or Google Play Advertising ID controls on Android), AdMob may process advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) or device-generated diagnostic signals.
  • Ad Delivery & Network Requests: Ad requests are fulfilled directly by Google AdMob according to the configured content rating parameter (General Audiences). The app does not integrate third-party mediation adapters or external ad exchange SDKs; ad delivery is handled directly through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Ad-Free Option: Players who purchase the optional Lifetime VIP Pass permanently disable banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad displays, terminating in-app ad requests.
  • Vendor Policies: For further details on advertising data processing, review Google Advertising Technologies.

4. In-App Purchases & Billing Information

Financial transactions for optional in-app purchases (such as the Lifetime VIP Pass) are handled entirely by platform operators: Apple via StoreKit 2 on iOS and Google via Google Play Billing on Android. The developer does not collect, receive, process, or store credit card numbers, banking details, or financial billing addresses.

5. Children's Privacy

Emoji Riddle is categorized as a casual puzzle game intended for general audiences aged 13+. Junior Mode provides visual and English pronunciation content designed for younger family members. In Picture Play mode, advertising displays are suppressed.

Because the App uses standard advertising and analytics SDKs on general audience distribution tracks, parents and guardians should note that network-level device telemetry is processed by third-party SDKs when the device is connected to the internet. We do not represent independent third-party COPPA certification. Families seeking an ad-free environment may utilize device parental controls or activate the Lifetime VIP Pass.

6. Data Management & Local Deletion

Because gameplay progress is stored locally in device preferences, you can reset or delete your local game progress at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the application storage in your device settings.
