Welcome to the official support hub for Emoji Riddle & Brain Teaser for iOS. We are committed to providing a fun, seamless, and high-quality puzzle-solving experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my VIP Pass? Open in-game Settings (⚙️ in the top left) and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged in with your original Apple ID, your Lifetime VIP Pass will be restored immediately at zero cost.
  • How does the Lifetime VIP Pass work? It is a one-time non-consumable purchase ($2.99) that permanently removes all ads, grants +1,000 bonus coins instantly, provides 2× coins on every solved puzzle, and refreshes 3 free VIP hints every single day.
  • Lost progress or stuck on a level? Use the "Reveal Letter" or "Remove 3" lifelines. Game progress is automatically saved to your device's local storage.

🔒 Privacy Policy GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy discloses how Emoji Riddle & Brain Teaser ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, handles, and protects your information.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect, require, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts. You do not need to create an account or provide any login credentials to play the game.

2. On-Device Game Data

All game state information—including your coin balance, completed puzzles, streak count, theme preferences, and daily VIP hint resets—is stored strictly on your local device via Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults). This data is never sent to external servers and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the application.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

All in-app purchases (such as the Lifetime VIP Pass) are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. We do not have access to, nor do we collect or store, your credit card details, payment instruments, or Apple ID credentials. Apple handles all billing and receipt validation under the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Telemetry (Google AdMob)

To keep the base game free to play, the App integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner ads, level completion interstitials, and opt-in rewarded video hints:

  • Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as IDFA if tracking authorization is granted via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, or vendor ID), general IP address for regional ad delivery, and crash/diagnostic metrics.
  • Users can opt out of personalized tracking via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad-Free Option: Purchasing the Lifetime VIP Pass completely disables all ad network initializations and eliminates 100% of banner, interstitial, and video advertisements.
  • For more details on Google's data practices, please consult the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Emoji Riddle & Brain Teaser is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
