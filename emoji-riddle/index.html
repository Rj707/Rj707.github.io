🔒 Privacy Policy GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Last Updated: October 2026

Your privacy is fundamental. This Privacy Policy discloses how Emoji Riddle — Brain Teaser ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, handles, and protects your information.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect, require, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or photos. You do not need an account or login credentials to play.

2. On-Device Game Data

All game progress—including your coin balance, completed puzzles, streak count, theme preferences, and daily VIP hint resets—is stored strictly on your local device via standard platform storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android). This data never leaves your device.

3. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases (such as the Lifetime VIP Pass) are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2 on iOS and Google via Google Play Billing on Android. We never access, collect, or store your payment card numbers or banking information.

4. Non-Intrusive Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the base game free to play, the App integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner ads, level completion interstitials, and opt-in rewarded video hints:

  • Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as IDFA under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, or Google Advertising ID on Android), coarse IP location for regional ad delivery, and diagnostic metrics.
  • Ad-Free Option: Purchasing the Lifetime VIP Pass permanently disables all advertising SDK initializations and removes 100% of ads.
  • For details on Google's advertising policies, consult the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Emoji Riddle is a family-friendly game designed for all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
