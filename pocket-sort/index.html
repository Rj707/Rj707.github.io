💬 Support & Feedback

Welcome to the official support hub for Pocket Sort. We are committed to providing a relaxing, responsive, and tactile color sorting puzzle experience across iOS and Android.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase? Open in-game Settings (⚙️ in the top left) and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged in with the Apple ID or Google account used to make the purchase, your ad-free entitlement will be restored immediately at zero cost.
  • How does the "Remove Ads" purchase work? It is a one-time non-consumable purchase ($1.99 USD or local equivalent) that permanently removes all banner ads, level transition interstitials, and app open ads across your device.
  • How do Hints and +1 Buffer Tubes work? Tap the Lightbulb icon in the top bar to calculate and demonstrate the optimal next move using our on-device Breadth-First Search (BFS) solver. If you find yourself in a tight spot, tap the +Tube button or accept a bonus vial during deadlock to add an extra holding slot.
  • How do I toggle themes or sound? Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) anytime to switch between Midnight Cosmos (Dark) and Warm Sand (Light) modes, adjust sound effects, or reset your move records.
  • Lost progress or switching devices? All level completions, move counts, and star badges are saved automatically to your device's local sandboxed storage.

🔒 Privacy Policy GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy discloses how Pocket Sort ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information and protects your rights.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect, require, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, contact lists, or photos. You can enjoy the complete game without creating an account or providing any credentials.

2. On-Device Game Data & 100% Offline Gameplay

All game progress—including your completed levels (1 to 30), fewest moves records, 3-star ratings, active puzzle state, and theme preferences—is stored strictly on your local device via sandboxed local storage (UserDefaults / SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and does not require an active internet connection to play.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit & Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit and Google via Google Play Billing. We never have access to, nor do we collect or store, your payment credentials, credit card numbers, or account passwords. Transactions are governed under the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Telemetry (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and keep the base game free to play, Pocket Sort integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve non-intrusive banner ads, occasional level transition interstitials (with strict 2-minute cooldowns), and optional rewarded assistance:

  • AdMob may process anonymous device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID or vendor ID) and coarse diagnostic telemetry solely for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and performance monitoring.
  • Ad-Free Option: Purchasing "Remove Ads" completely disables all ad network initializations and eliminates 100% of banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements.
  • For more information on Google's advertising practices, please review the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Pocket Sort is a family-friendly game designed for puzzle lovers of all ages. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy standards. Ad requests are flagged for child-directed treatment with maximum G ratings.

6. Data Deletion Instructions

Because Pocket Sort does not maintain remote user accounts or store personal data on cloud servers, deleting your data is instantaneous and completely under your control:

  • In-App Reset: Open Settings (⚙️) → tap "Reset Progress" to immediately wipe all local level history, stars, and move statistics.
  • Uninstalling: Deleting the app from your device automatically removes all sandboxed files and local data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
