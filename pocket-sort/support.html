💬 Support & Feedback

Welcome to the official support hub for Pocket Sort. We are committed to providing a relaxing, responsive, and tactile color sorting puzzle experience across iOS and Android.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase? Open in-game Settings (⚙️ in the top left) and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged in with the Apple ID or Google account used to make the purchase, your ad-free entitlement will be restored immediately at zero cost.
  • How does the "Remove Ads" purchase work? It is a one-time non-consumable purchase ($1.99 USD or local equivalent) that permanently removes all banner ads, level transition interstitials, and app open ads across your device.
  • How do Hints and +1 Buffer Tubes work? Tap the Lightbulb icon in the top bar to calculate and demonstrate the optimal next move using our on-device Breadth-First Search (BFS) solver. If you find yourself in a tight spot, tap the +Tube button or accept a bonus vial during deadlock to add an extra holding slot.
  • How do I toggle themes or sound? Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) anytime to switch between Midnight Cosmos (Dark) and Warm Sand (Light) modes, adjust sound effects, or reset your move records.
  • Lost progress or switching devices? All level completions, move counts, and star badges are saved automatically to your device's local sandboxed storage.
